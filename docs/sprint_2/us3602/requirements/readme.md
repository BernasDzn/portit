## 3. Acceptance Criteria:

<details><summary>Show acceptance criteria</summary>
<p>

- The team must define what constitutes a personal data breach.
- The team must understand and document the notification requirements involving both the competent supervisory authority and the affected data subjects, as well as what information those notifications must contain.
- The team must identify the deadline for notifying a data breach that poses a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms.

</p>
</details> 


## 4. Business Value
> Establishing clear data breach handling procedures ensures rapid, compliant response to security incidents, minimizing legal exposure and reputational damage. Understanding notification requirements and deadlines enables timely communication with authorities and affected individuals, demonstrating accountability and regulatory compliance. Documented procedures reduce confusion during crisis situations, support coordinated incident response, and help maintain stakeholder trust. Preparedness for breach scenarios protects organizational interests and individual rights.

## 5. Definition of Ready
> This US follows the defined [global definition of ready](../../../global_docs/def_of_ready.md)

## 6. Definition of Done
- Personal data breach is clearly defined
- Notification requirements for supervisory authority are documented
- Notification requirements for affected data subjects are documented
- Required information content for notifications is specified
- Notification deadline for risky breaches is identified
- The implementation checks all acceptance criteria
