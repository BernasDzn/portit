## 3. Acceptance Criteria:

<details><summary>Show acceptance criteria</summary>
<p>

- The scene must include at least ambient and directional lighting to ensure visibility of all 3D objects.
- Lighting should enhance depth perception and object contours without causing overexposure or darkness.
- Shadows or highlights must be used without causing significant performance degradation.
- The illumination setup must work consistently across zoom levels and camera angles.

</p>
</details> 


## 4. Business Value
> Proper lighting ensures all 3D objects remain visible and interpretable regardless of viewing angle or scene complexity. Strategic use of ambient and directional lighting enhances depth perception, reveals object contours, and creates realistic visual hierarchy without compromising performance. Consistent illumination across different viewing conditions ensures users can reliably understand spatial relationships and object characteristics, supporting accurate operational decisions.

> This US follows the defined [global definition of ready](../../../global_docs/def_of_ready.md)

## 6. Definition of Done
- Scene includes ambient and directional lighting
- All 3D objects are clearly visible
- Lighting enhances depth perception and object contours
- No overexposure or excessive darkness occurs
- Shadows or highlights are implemented without performance degradation
- Lighting works consistently across zoom levels and camera angles
- The implementation checks all acceptance criteria
